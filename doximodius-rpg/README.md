# Doximodius

A compact playable interpretation joining the Sir Bedivere narrative to the earlier adventure map and the later RPG rules. The notebook establishes Doximodius, the sentient sword Dracobrane, statistics, party storage, monsters, treasure, six spell circles, action phases, and environmental features.

The reconstruction expands those notes into a directed ten-location adventure. Encounters are placed in specific rooms, so the route and risks are readable. The full game is navigable without a mouse: arrows/WASD or a gamepad D-pad move between connected rooms, Tab or gamepad B cycles the room's targets, I inspects, and Enter or gamepad A uses the selected object. In combat, Enter/A attacks; C/X casts; B-key/gamepad B braces; E/LB evades; P/Y drinks a potion; and F3/RB uses the rescue scroll. The blue crystal or a lightning spell opens the Great Door; a record and mirror explain the dragon's weakness and attack rhythm; a fire shield helps against its breath; Dracobrane is required to harm it. Sir Gareth's joust is the final challenge, with the choice to seal the sword instead. The pack, rescue scroll, potions, magic, innkeeper, safe resting place, treasure, and level progression give the player further options.

The display is a 320×240 4:3 playfield for a CRT-like presentation. Press `T` or use the button below the screen to switch between the crisp C64 text treatment and a larger, smoother modern typeface; the pixel-art world remains unchanged.

An original three-voice-style fantasy theme uses rapid chord tones, pulse lead, triangle bass and noise percussion. Combat, magic, movement, healing, victory and defeat have synthesized effects; audio starts on the first key press and `M` toggles mute.
