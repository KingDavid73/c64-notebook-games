# Collection classification

The notebook pages have been classified by their internal subject matter rather than by camera order. Original photographs and generated contact sheets remain in the local workspace and are intentionally excluded from this public repository.

## Insect Attack

Nineteen pages covering the crop-spraying premise, scrolling farmland, five insects, movement and collision flowcharts, 6502 routines, title/status layout, and helicopter sprite.

## Doximodius RPG

Nineteen pages combining the earlier adventure location graph with the Sir Bedivere narrative and a custom RPG implementation: statistics, experience levels, party disk persistence, movement, monsters, action phases, spells, treasure, and environmental features. This material is internally consistent enough to identify it as an original RPG rather than a Bard's Tale campaign.

## Forest Run

Four pages defining forest foreground/background sprites, rocks, logs, trees, birds, obstacle movement and collisions, treasure scoring, and a randomly moving bear.

## C64 Utility Suite

Four pages describing disk utilities, character and sprite editors, sound/music tools, small games, and educational programs.

## Archive

Generated mixed contact sheets are kept locally for archival purposes and are not part of this public repository.

## Possible spaceship game

The submitted notebook pages and archived contact sheets were reviewed while sorting the projects. They document Insect Attack, Doximodius, Forest Run, and the C64 Utility Suite, but do not show a distinct spaceship game's premise, controls, enemy or weapon rules, or level structure. The Utility Suite does mention a few small games, but its notes are not enough to identify one as a space game. No separate spaceship project folder has been created, so the archive does not imply a design that is not present in the source pages.
