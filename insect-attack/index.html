THE NOTEBOOK STORY

You are the pilot of a crop-spraying helicopter in Kansas, fighting the little insects that make farming so difficult. But now, over your radio you hear that a problem in the chemicals department at the nearest fertilization distributor has caused a very strange effect on the bugs. The little pests have mutated into huge bugs the size of your helicopter!

You quickly go to the chemicals department and ask them what is going on. They tell you they have 3 helicopters equipped with a special poison made into a spray. Having only a short time to concoct the super-powerful bug spray, it isn’t very effective. It only works when you hold it on them for a long time, but barely enough time to get out of the bugs’ way!

You, being undaunted, take off in the first of these copters ready to make the biggest notch in the bug infestation you can.
